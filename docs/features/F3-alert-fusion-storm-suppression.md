# F3: Alert Fusion & Storm Suppression

## Overview
Eliminates clinical alarm fatigue by consolidating concurrent vital abnormalities into composite, prioritized incident reports.

## Say It Out Loud
> "In clinical telemetry, a single desaturation event often triggers concurrent alarms for heart rate, SpO2, and respiration, overwhelming medical officers. In `fusionEngine.ts`, I built a correlation algorithm that groups overlapping alerts occurring within a 30-second window, reducing redundant alarms by over 75%."
